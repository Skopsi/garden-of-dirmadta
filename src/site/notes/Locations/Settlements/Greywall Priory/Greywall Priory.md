In the deepest part of the woods lurks a grey-stoned priory with a pointed roof and arched, stained glass windows. Around 40 Knights of St. Ydris live here led by grim [[Characters/NPCs/Inquisitor Justinia Morvin\|Inquisitor Justinia Morvin]]. Few know 160-year-old St. Ydris is still alive deep within the priory's catacombs, bound painfully (and willingly) in a magic circle to trap the mighty demon lord who possesses him. Initiates into the order gain their fell powers by drinking of his cursed blood and surviving. These knights swear to use their fiendish abilities to hunt demons, never losing control or embracing evil. Thus their oath: "I will walk the path between darkness and dawn, ever vigilant, sword drawn." They are sorely outnumbered.
