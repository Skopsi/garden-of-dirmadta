300 fisherfolk live in a motte and-bailey hold where the river meets the bog. A palisade surrounds the village, and [[Characters/NPCs/Reeve Tarley Winters\|Reeve Tarley Winters]] resides in the keep atop a hill with around 30 guards. The village is still recovering from an attack by six [[Bestiary/Skrell\|skrell]] demons who breached the palisade and slew 20 souls. Winters believes sorcery attracts the demons. Villagers eye each other warily, and the gossips at The Crayfish Tavern wonder who will be accused next.
