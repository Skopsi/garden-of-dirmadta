# Description
The oldest surviving, fish-reeking tavern in [[Locations/Settlements/Vansen's Hold/Vansen's Hold\|Vansen's Hold]]. Age shows in every beam and board.
The main room is wide and open. A long, splintered bar stretches across the back wall, worn from years of use. Tables sit along the edges and in the corners. The regulars keep their spots. Everyone knows better than to take them. The air smells of brine and old wood. Floorboards creak underfoot. Low, gruff voices fill the space. Upstairs, a small second floor offers simple rooms. Just enough for the few travelers who pass through [[Locations/Settlements/Vansen's Hold/Vansen's Hold\|Vansen's Hold]].
