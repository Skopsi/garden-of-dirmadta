A scribe in [[Locations/Settlements/Vansen's Hold/Vansen's Hold\|Vansen's Hold]] Library. 

His pale skin is almost translucent with age; Though unlike his brother, it bares no blemishes or marks save for the imprint of his glasses.
He paces with a permanent hunched posture but speaks with a youthful exuberance. 
His formal robes reflect the faded grandeur of a position that may have once held significance in the past.

Related to [[Lore/NPCs/Garian Ledis\|Garian Ledis]] (Brother).
