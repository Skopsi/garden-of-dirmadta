A member of the [[Lore/Factions/Knights of St. Ydris\|Knights of St. Ydris]].
Spends most of his time in the Greywall Priory's infirmary caring for knights who have taken on injuries (and survived).

He is the oldest surviving member, his days of venturing outside the priory are long past.
Beyond a branded letter W on his forehead, his face bears several scars and wrinkles hidden behind sparse white bushy eyebrows and facial hair. He is missing his left index finger.


Garian is related to [[Lore/NPCs/Harus Ledis\|Harus Ledis]] (Brother).
