Leader of the [[Lore/Factions/Knights of St. Ydris\|Knights of St. Ydris]], he spends most of his time reviewing reports and delegating work to the knights.

His voice is calm but cutting, sharpened by years of rooting out heresy and corruption. Though as years have past, his demeanor has eased. Some say this is due to age, others portend fleeting hope.

He stands tall and steady at all times, as if a statue brought to life. He has well-kept greying hair and a short beard.
