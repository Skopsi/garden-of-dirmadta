A member of the [[Lore/Factions/Knights of St. Ydris\|Knights of St. Ydris]], she is a cook and smithy for the order.
She has a small wiry frame, and her thin hair is often a knotted mess.
Her speech is scratchy and impetuous. She can be slightly inattentive at times.

She likes to keep busy and can often be found patrolling outside the priory when there is nothing to repair.
