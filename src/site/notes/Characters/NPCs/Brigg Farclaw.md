Leader of the [[Lore/Factions/Golden Maggots\|Golden Maggots]], a gang of outsiders that are known to roam the further reaches of [[Makdain\|Makdain]] in [[Dirmadta\|Dirmadta]]. Most often hired for mercenary work or any sort of odd jobs tied to Brigg's contacts.

A broad-shouldered, bald man with scruffy facial hair and two gold-capped canines, his harsh voice carries quiet authority. Slightly unassuming at a glance, he leads with a steady hand. 
Fiercely protective of his crew and respected for it.
