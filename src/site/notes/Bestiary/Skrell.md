With hooked barbed claws they prowl on all fours. Tight pale skin stretched over thin jagged bones all about marked by slim patches of matted fur. Their eyes are bulbous and rolling and they slink out of the blackened marrow trees like melted wax.
They are born feral, they will live ravenous, and will die pitifully.
